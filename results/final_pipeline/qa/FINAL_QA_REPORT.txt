4. REASONS & RATIONALE FOR DESIGN CHOICES
================================================================================
1. Production Baseline Freezing:
   The APTOS ResNet-50 baseline exhibits superior sensitivity (96.64%) on rural screening distributions at threshold 0.24. Retraining or replacing it with experimental combined models would introduce regression risk. Therefore, Combined V1, Combined V2, Localization, UNet V3, and DRIVE UNet are kept strictly experimental.
2. Threshold Selection (0.24):
   Operating at 0.24 prioritizes patient safety in public health screening by minimizing false negatives (missing true diabetic retinopathy), achieving 96.64% sensitivity.
3. Post-Hoc Temperature Scaling:
   T = 0.9215 calibrates the model probabilities to reflect true diagnostic likelihood without altering classifier decision boundaries or weights.
4. Parametric Capacity Modeling:
   Throughput is calculated mathematically from mean pipeline latency (325.2 ms) to provide an engineering sizing model (11,070 pts/hr nominal).

5. KNOWN LIMITATIONS & CLINICAL SCOPE
================================================================================
1. Clinical Validation:
   NOT PERFORMED. NetraSetu is an AI research prototype and clinical decision-support tool. It is not CE-marked, FDA-cleared, or approved by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO).
2. Domain Specificity:
   - Optic Disc and Fovea localization models were trained and validated on IDRiD images; performance on other camera types may vary.
   - Lesion segmentation (UNet V3) was trained and evaluated on IDRiD images.
   - Vessel segmentation was trained and evaluated on DRIVE images.
   - Cross-domain inferences carry explicit research disclaimers.
3. Microaneurysm Segmentation Weakness:
   Microaneurysm (MA) segmentation achieves low Dice (0.0311) due to extreme pixel sparsity (~0.01% of retinal image pixels) and single-pixel annotation noise. MA results should be interpreted as localized attention cues rather than quantitative volume measurements.
4. Capacity Simulator Disclaimer:
   Throughput and compute utilization figures (0.45% for 100,000 screenings/yr, 1.13% for 250,000 screenings/yr) and triage cost-offset models (₹28.8 Lakhs) are parametric engineering estimates based on configured timing and case-mix assumptions. They do not represent measured clinical deployment capacity.
