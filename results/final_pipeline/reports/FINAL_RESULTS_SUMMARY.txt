The major software and research components of Problem Statement 26038 have been 
implemented and regression-tested, while clinical validation remains outside the 
current scope. NetraSetu functions as an AI-assisted research screening prototype 
combining automated Image Quality Gating, 5-class DR classification, post-hoc confidence 
calibration, Grad-CAM attention maps, anatomical landmark localization, lesion 
segmentation, retinal vessel extraction, and automated report generation.
