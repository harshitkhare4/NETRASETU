Scientific Principles of Grad-CAM in NetraSetu

  • Target Layer: model.layer4[-1] of ResNet-50, which captures rich spatial representations before global average pooling.
  • Resolution Harmony: ResNet-50 classifies at 224×224; Grad-CAM activation is bilinearly upsampled to 512×512 to preserve anatomical detail on high-res displays.
  • Targeted Class Gradients: Computes gradients strictly with respect to the argmax predicted DR class logit via ClassifierOutputTarget.

Clinical Interpretation Guidelines

  • Attention Visualization: Warmer red/yellow colors indicate image regions that increased the model's confidence for the predicted severity.
  • Non-Histological: Grad-CAM demonstrates AI attention patterns; it does NOT constitute automated micro-histology or definitive lesion confirmation.
  • Auditability: Explainability heatmaps are stored alongside every screening for ophthalmologist retrospective validation.

Problem Statement (ID 26038)

Diabetic Retinopathy is a leading cause of preventable blindness in India. With over 77 million diabetic individuals and a severe shortage of ophthalmologists in rural regions, NetraSetu bridges this diagnostic gap by providing rapid, explainable AI screening at the point of care.

MATLAB / Simulink Disclosure

The original problem statement references MATLAB/Simulink. Our current executable prototype is implemented in Python/PyTorch + Flask. MATLAB and Simulink system-level workflow models can be represented separately for data throughput, hardware latency, and pipeline analysis.
